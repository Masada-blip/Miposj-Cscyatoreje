# Mēpoßj Cßcyatoreje
**Mēpoßj Cßcyatoreje** is an a posteriori constructed language developed for an original fictional universe. 
Created since age 11, it blends Finno-Ugric and Germanic phonology with Slavic inflectional elements, 
resulting in an agglutinative morphology with characteristic consonant clusters at morpheme boundaries.

The language features:
- **20 cases** across nominative, spatial, possessive, and benefactive domains
- **3 tenses** (present, past, future)
- **2 writing systems** (non-Latin script and Latinized)
- **Multiple dialects** reflecting regional and temporal variation

The language embodies the aesthetic of a human tongue evolved in far-future space—familiar yet alien.

# External History

The creation of Mēpoßj Cßcyatoreje stemmed not from a specific concept, commission, or linguistic experiment, 
but from simple curiosity and boredom—a teenager seeking creative outlet.

**Early Development (Foundation)**
The initial approach combined pragmatism with innovation. Existing words were borrowed from Finno-Ugric 
and Germanic languages, either through translation blending or selective adaptation. Parallel to this, 
entirely original words were coined without etymological constraint.

The grammatical skeleton followed a deliberate design principle: **elimination of prepositions in favor 
of a case system**. This led to the development of tenses and the foundational case structures that 
would define the language's agglutinative character.

**Orthographic Reform & Morphological Expansion**
Following the establishment of core grammar, the language underwent orthographic standardization, 
introducing the dual writing systems. Subsequently, a rich system of affixes and prefixes was developed, 
allowing greater morphological flexibility and derivational depth.

**Modern Revision (Standardization)**
Decades of development culminated in comprehensive grammatical review. Redundancies were eliminated, 
inconsistencies corrected, and the lexicon refined toward greater academic rigor—a dramatic shift 
from the youthful intuition of its earliest iterations.
